# Riggle

## 0.1 — initial playable editor bake
- Eight primary landmarks; independently editable/mirrored 13-marker biped.
- Scene view joint handle, clickable position placement and preview skeleton.
- Bounds-based landmark starting positions.
- Twenty-bone Unity deformation hierarchy (fingers and face omitted).
- Non-destructive mesh cloning, saved bind poses and 4-weight skinning.
- Topology-local triangle diffusion and adjustable region envelopes.
- Optional validated Unity Humanoid Avatar build.
- Built-in-primitives demo character through the GameObject menu.
- Undo and original-renderer restoration.

Known limits: no volumetric heat weights, no rig controller, no IK, no non-humanoid template yet; project-side Unity Editor test pending.
